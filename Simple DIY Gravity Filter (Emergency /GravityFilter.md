1️⃣ Simple DIY Gravity Filter (Emergency / Outdoor)

You need:

Plastic bottle (cut in half)

Cloth / coffee filter

Activated charcoal (or crushed charcoal from clean wood)

Sand (fine + coarse)

Gravel / small stones

Layer order (bottom → top):

Cloth / coffee filter

Activated charcoal

Fine sand

Coarse sand

Gravel

How it works:

Gravel → removes big debris

Sand → removes smaller particles

Charcoal → removes odors, some chemicals

Pour water slowly from the top, collect at the bottom.

2️⃣ Bottle + Cloth (Very Basic)

You need:

Bottle

Clean cloth / cotton

Steps:

Stretch cloth over bottle opening

Pour water slowly

✅ Removes dirt
❌ Does NOT remove bacteria or chemicals

3️⃣ Charcoal Filter Only (Taste Improvement)

You need:

Activated charcoal

Cloth

Bottle or tube

Good for:

Smell

Taste

Some organic contaminants

Not enough for biological safety.

4️⃣ Make Water SAFE After Filtering (CRITICAL)

After filtering, do at least one of these:

🔥 Boiling (best)

Boil 1–3 minutes (3 min at high altitude)

💧 Chemical disinfection

Chlorine tablets

Iodine tablets (not for long-term use)

☀️ Solar (SODIS)

Clear PET bottle

Full sun 6–8 hours

5️⃣ What DIY Filters CANNOT Remove

❌ Viruses
❌ Heavy metals (lead, mercury)
❌ Salts (not desalination)
❌ Industrial toxins (reliably)
