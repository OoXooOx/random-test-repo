Here’s a simple, reliable way to make crispy fried potatoes (pan-fried style 🍳). I’ll give you a base version + pro tips so you can tweak it.

🥔 Classic Fried Potatoes (Crispy Outside, Soft Inside)
4
🧾 Ingredients
4–5 medium potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet work best)
3–4 tbsp oil (vegetable or sunflower)
1 tbsp butter (optional but 🔥 for flavor)
Salt (to taste)
Black pepper
Optional:
Garlic (2–3 cloves)
Onion
Paprika / chili flakes
Fresh herbs (parsley, dill)
