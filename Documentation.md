Hearty Vegetable Soup (Serves 4–6)
Ingredients

Base

2 tbsp olive oil

1 large onion, diced

2 carrots, sliced

2 celery stalks, sliced

3 cloves garlic, minced

Vegetables

2 potatoes, diced (or sweet potatoes)

1 zucchini, chopped

1 bell pepper, chopped

1 cup green beans (fresh or frozen)

1 can (400 g / 14 oz) diced tomatoes

Protein & Heft

1 can (400 g) beans (kidney, white, chickpeas, or lentils), drained & rinsed

½ cup pasta or ½ cup rice or extra potatoes

Liquid & Seasoning

1.5–2 liters (6–8 cups) vegetable broth (or water + bouillon)

1 tsp salt (adjust to taste)

½ tsp black pepper

1 tsp smoked paprika

1 tsp dried thyme or oregano

1 bay leaf

Finish (optional but recommended)

A handful of chopped parsley or dill

A squeeze of lemon juice or splash of vinegar
