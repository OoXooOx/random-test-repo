# How to Buy a Ticket to Space

Buying a space ticket depends on what kind of trip you want. A short suborbital flight is the simplest real option. An orbital trip is much more expensive and requires serious training.

## Simple Buying Steps

1. Choose the flight type: balloon, suborbital rocket, or orbital mission.
2. Contact the provider through its official website.
3. Ask for current seat price, deposit, refund rules, launch window, and medical rules.
4. Pass medical screening and background checks.
5. Sign contracts and pay the deposit.
6. Complete training before the flight.
7. Travel to the launch site and fly.

## Notes

- Prices change often and may not be public.
- Suborbital trips are usually minutes in space.
- Orbital missions can last days or weeks and cost much more.
- Use official provider websites only. Avoid random brokers unless the company confirms them.
