1. Choose the Right Mountain

Start small and non-technical.

Beginner: well-marked hiking trails, no ropes needed

Advanced: snow, ice, steep rock, glaciers (needs training)

Rule: If you’re asking how, start with a hike, not a climb.

2. Get Basic Fitness

You should be able to:

Walk uphill 3–6 hours with a backpack

Climb stairs continuously for 30–60 minutes

Train by:

Hiking hills

Stair climbing

Squats & lunges

Cardio (running, cycling)

3. Essential Gear (Beginner)

Sturdy hiking boots (ankle support)

Backpack (20–30 L)

Water (2–3 liters)

Food (high-energy snacks)

Weather-appropriate clothing (layers!)

Hat & sunscreen

Map or offline GPS

Headlamp

Basic first-aid kit
